Show me how

1. Just say it

"Make me a website about sharks." That is a complete instruction. You do not need to know any special words.

2. It gets built

Colours, fonts and layout are chosen for you by a design brain that knows what looks good. You do not have to pick.

3. Change your mind

"Make it blue." "Add a photo of a shark." "The title is too small." Say it and watch it change.
